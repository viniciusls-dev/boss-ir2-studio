# Example presets

Use **Import preset JSON (`Importar preset JSON…`)** in IR-2 Studio to add one of these files to your local library. Importing does not send settings to the pedal. With pedal editing enabled, use **Apply preset to pedal (`Aplicar preset ao pedal`)** to write both channels. Existing settings on both pedal channels will be replaced.

## Someday — Clean / Brit

An original starting point inspired by the clean/heavy contrast in Nickelback's *Someday*. This is not an official artist preset or a recreation of the recording. Listen with your own guitar and speakers or headphones, then adjust the levels and tone. The preset assumes the factory cabinet IRs for CLEAN and BRIT; it does not install or replace IRs or change global settings.

| Control (0–127) | Green | Red |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amp | CLEAN | BRIT |
| Gain | 42 | 78 |
| Bass | 58 | 56 |
| Middle | 64 | 76 |
| Treble | 72 | 66 |
| Level | 90 | 80 |
| Ambience | 24 | 14 |

Use the clean channel for the softer parts, with the external overdrive off. For the heavier parts, try the bridge pickup and a Tone Ink Cold Shot before the IR-2 INPUT. Suggested starting positions: Gain/Drive at 9 o'clock, Bass at 10:30, Mid fully clockwise, Treble at noon. Start Volume at unity (the same loudness on and off), then add a small boost; 1 o'clock is a position to try, not a calibrated boost. If your version has switches, start with Comp GLORY and Mid Cut MARSH. Switching IR-2 channels does not switch the external overdrive.

The [manufacturer's Cold Shot description](https://www.toneink.com.br/produtos/cold-shot-clear-overdrive-envio-15-02-y9gcb/) explains that Mid at maximum leaves its passive mid cut inactive, high Bass settings can become fuzzy, and GLORY offers the most headroom of its clipping modes. These suggestions assume that version of the pedal. Adjust to suit other versions.

Balance the two channels by ear at your usual playing volume. Their Level numbers do not guarantee equal perceived loudness. The preset notes also include these playing and adjustment tips in Portuguese.
