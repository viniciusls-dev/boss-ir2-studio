IR-2 Studio — macOS Preview

REQUIREMENTS
- A Mac with an Apple Silicon processor (M1 or later).
- macOS 26 or later.
- The BOSS IR-2 driver for macOS, for USB pedal control.
  Official downloads: https://www.boss.info/global/support/by_product/ir-2/updates_drivers/

INSTALL
1. Open the DMG and drag IR2Studio to Applications.
   If you downloaded the ZIP, extract it and move IR2Studio.app to Applications.
2. Open IR2Studio from Applications.

FIRST OPEN
This free preview uses a local signature and has not been notarized by Apple.
macOS may block its first launch. If you trust this download, follow Apple's
instructions for allowing this specific app in System Settings > Privacy & Security:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

CONNECT THE PEDAL
1. Power the IR-2 normally and connect an instrument cable to INPUT.
2. Use USB mode VENDOR, install the BOSS driver, and connect a USB data cable.
3. Open Meu IR-2 (My IR-2). The app starts in read-only mode.
4. Switch on Editar pedal (Edit pedal) to send changes.

KNOW BEFORE YOU PLAY
- Changes from the pedal are received in real time; there is no 3-second polling.
- Atualizar (Refresh) reads both channels again when needed.
- Green/Red cards inspect a channel. Selecionar (Select) changes the pedal's selection.
- Channel selection does not turn the effect on. Check the physical CHECK LED:
  green/red means the effect is on. An unlit LED can mean bypass.
- Save as preset captures both channels; applying a preset sends both channels.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese are supported. The app follows your Mac's language.
  Choose Language (Idioma) in the sidebar to override it, then quit and reopen the app.
  Preset names and notes are kept as written.

This preview does not update firmware, load cabinet IRs, restore full backups,
or read/control bypass. It is not an official BOSS or Roland product.
